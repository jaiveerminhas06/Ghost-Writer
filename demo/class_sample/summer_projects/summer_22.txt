The roles of teachers and students are crucial in shaping one's academic journey. Teachers are responsible for guiding students through their educational path, while students are expected to perform well in order to graduate. However, the success of both teachers and students relies heavily on their relationship with each other. If students feel comfortable discussing their difficulties with their teachers, teachers should provide support and assistance to help them succeed.

One aspect of this relationship is the design of projects. While students may have their own ideas, it is important to ensure that the project is challenging and promotes learning. If a project is too easy, students will not be pushed to think critically and may not learn anything new. On the other hand, if a project is too difficult, students may become discouraged and give up entirely.

In a group project, it is important to have a balance of smart and lazy students. Smart students can help the group complete the project efficiently, while lazy students may cheat or contribute little to the project. If the teacher designs the project, they can ensure that it is challenging and promotes learning, while also providing feedback on how the students can improve their performance.

As a student, I can relate to the idea of not completing an assignment during break. However, it is important to finish the project before it is due, as it will help improve grades and overall academic performance. Teachers can also help students stay on track by providing deadlines and expectations for each stage of the project.

In conclusion, teachers and students both play important roles in each other's academic success. While students may have their own ideas, it is important to ensure that the project is challenging and promotes learning. Teachers can provide feedback and guidance to help students improve their performance and achieve their goals.
