The flag line is a choice, not a fact

It is the score that about 0.5% (strict) or 1% (standard) of student essays reached in validation. It was set on familiar topics. On new topics, more honest essays cross it.

A flag is a reason to ask questions, not evidence.

It learned to separate US school essays, grades 6 to 12, from essays written by 2023-era AI models. Nobody has measured how it treats:

  • Writers whose first language isn't English
  • Adults, or any genre other than school essays
  • Newer AI models, and humaniser tools that rewrite whole paragraphs
  • Essays that are only partly AI-written
