Type

Space and radius

2 px steps up to 16 (for gaps next to type), 4 px steps to 32, then 8 and 16. Radii grow with an element's size and importance; nothing shares one radius by default.

Strict

Standard

Concentric radii: 8 px inside + 4 px inset = 12 px outside here, and 8 + 6 = 14 for the example list. When padding is larger than the outer radius (cards with 20 px padding), the corners are far enough apart that matching them doesn't read, so inner controls keep their own radius.

Depth

Each shadow is a tight contact layer plus a wide soft layer, tinted with the desk's blue instead of black. Raised elements get a 1 px inset highlight on top. There are no gradients, so no grain is needed to hide banding.

Raised, sh-1

Buttons, the selected segment. Inset top 55% white, 0 1 1 at 10%, 0 2 6 −2 at 14%.

Resting paper, sh-2

The essay, the verdict panel. Inset top 80% white, 0 1 1 at 8%, 0 10 28 −10 at 20%.

Floating, sh-3

Popovers, toasts. 0 1 2 at 12%, 0 28 64 −18 at 32%.

Motion

Only transform and opacity animate. One moment gets a spring: the score marker landing on the ruler. Under reduced motion, movement becomes an opacity change of 120 ms or less.
